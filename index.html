Our Story

A Passion for Food, A Love for Belgrade

LaRoc is Belgrade's destination for those who believe that dining is more than just a meal — it's a moment to be savoured. Nestled in the heart of the city at Mirka Sandića 1, we welcome guests into a warm, sophisticated space where great food, genuine hospitality, and an unhurried atmosphere come together seamlessly.

From our celebrated LaRoc Breakfast and stone-baked artisan pizzas to premium grill cuts, hand-rolled fresh pasta, and indulgent desserts, every dish is crafted daily using quality ingredients from trusted local producers. Whether you're meeting a friend for morning coffee or celebrating a special occasion over dinner, LaRoc sets the stage for memories worth making.
